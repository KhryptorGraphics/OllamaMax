# Training Satisfaction Scores

**Last Updated:** 2025-10-27

## Overview

This document presents user satisfaction metrics and feedback for the OllamaMax Distributed Training System based on participant surveys, test results, and user feedback. Satisfaction metrics provide direct feedback on training quality, user experience, and learning effectiveness.

## Purpose

User satisfaction tracking enables the training team to:
- Measure learner satisfaction across all training modules
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement
- Validate training content effectiveness
- Prioritize enhancement efforts
- Demonstrate training program value

### Net Promoter Score (NPS)
**Definition:** Likelihood to recommend training (0-10 scale)

- **Score:** 55
- **Target:** ≥ 30 (Industry Standard)
- **Status:** ✅ Excellent
- **Industry Benchmark:** 30-50 (Good), 50+ (Excellent)

**NPS Distribution:**
- **Promoters (9-10):** 65%
- **Passives (7-8):** 30%
- **Detractors (0-6):** 5%

**Calculation:** NPS = (% Promoters - % Detractors) = 65% - 5% = 55

**Collection Method:** Single question survey: "How likely are you to recommend this training to a colleague?" (0-10 scale)
**Reporting Cadence:** Monthly calculation with trend analysis

## Data Collection Methods

### Post-Module Surveys
**Distribution:** Automatically triggered upon module completion
**Format:** 5-8 questions per module
**Response Time:** 2-3 minutes

**Sample Questions:**
1. How would you rate the overall quality of this module? (1-5)
2. How easy was this module to follow? (1-5)
3. How valuable will this knowledge be in your work? (1-5)
4. How likely are you to recommend this training? (0-10)
5. What did you like most about this module? (open-ended)
6. What could be improved? (open-ended)

### Program Completion Survey
**Distribution:** Triggered upon completing all modules
**Format:** 12-15 questions
**Response Time:** 5-7 minutes

**Key Areas:**
- Overall satisfaction (1-5)
- Content quality rating (1-5)
- Ease of use rating (1-5)
- Practical value rating (1-5)
- Technical execution rating (1-5)
- Learning effectiveness rating (1-5)
- Likelihood to recommend (0-10)
- Open-ended feedback

### Follow-Up Survey (30 Days Post-Completion)
**Purpose:** Measure long-term satisfaction and knowledge retention
**Distribution:** Email 30 days after program completion
**Response Time:** 3-5 minutes

**Key Questions:**
1. Have you applied the skills learned? (Yes/No)
2. How frequently? (Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Rarely)
3. How useful have the skills been? (1-5)
4. Would you still recommend this training? (0-10)

## Thresholds and Alerts

### Critical (Immediate Action Required)
- Overall satisfaction < 3.0/5
- Module satisfaction < 2.5/5
- NPS < 0
- Multiple negative comments in short period

### Warning (Action Recommended)
- Overall satisfaction 3.0-3.5/5
- Module satisfaction 2.5-3.5/5
- NPS 0-20
- Response rate < 50%

### Good Standing
- Overall satisfaction ≥ 4.0/5
- Module satisfaction ≥ 4.0/5
- NPS ≥ 30
- Response rate ≥ 70%
